TOEIC Part 7: Inquiry Letter

Original TOEIC-style practice | Five questions | B1–B2

Read the inquiry letter and select the best answer to each question.

To: sales@modernoffice.com
From: purchasing@brightstar.com
Subject: Inquiry Regarding Office Equipment

Dear Sales Representative,

Our company is planning to open a new branch office in December, and we are interested in purchasing office equipment from your company.

We would like to request a quotation for 25 printers and 40 computer monitors. Please include information about available models, unit prices, and any discounts for large orders.

In addition, could you inform us about your delivery schedule and warranty conditions? We would prefer to receive the equipment no later than November 20 so that our staff can complete the installation before the office opens.

If your prices and terms are competitive, we may consider placing additional orders next year.

We look forward to your prompt response.

Sincerely,
David Chen
Purchasing Department
Brightstar Corporation
